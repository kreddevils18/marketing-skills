# Loop Marketing in brief

HubSpot introduced Loop Marketing in September 2025 as a replacement for the
linear funnel: a repeating cycle of four stages where each round of results
feeds the next. Humans own strategy, taste and final approval; AI does the
volume work (research, drafts, segmentation, analysis). Summary in our own
words; the sources are listed at the end.

## The four stages

| Stage | Question it answers | What it produces | Metrics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Express | Who are we, and what do we say that others do not? | Ideal customer profile, style guide, 3 to 5 contrarian beliefs, voice bank, campaign concepts | Time from idea to approved content, edits needed for voice, consistency |
| Tailor | How do we say it to this specific slice? | Segments from behavior and intent signals, personalized messages, formats per slice | Click-through and engagement per segment, conversion lift, audience growth, unsubscribes |
| Amplify | Where does it need to show up to be found? | Distribution across social, communities, video, creators, and visibility in AI search answers | Conversion per channel, brand mentions, citations in AI answers |
| Evolve | What worked, and what changes next round? | Predictions before launch, live monitoring, A/B tests on hooks, offers and audiences, lessons | Experiments per month, qualified leads, share of insights acted on |

## How a solo builder runs it

- **Express is the foundation.** Without a Taste Profile, AI output sounds like
  everyone else's. In files: `shared-rules.md` and `taste-profiles/<brand>.md`
  (marketing-project-setup).
- **A campaign is one turn of the loop.** Its brief has one section per stage
  (marketing-campaign-setup). Express inside a campaign is the concept test.
- **Tailor for a solo account** usually means picking one audience slice and
  the format that slice responds to, not building segments in a CRM.
- **Amplify** starts with one main channel and a repurposing plan; add channels
  only when the first one works.
- **Evolve** is the scheduled check-in. Its lessons go back into the profile
  (Express) and the next brief.

## Where to start

Start with the stage that has the biggest gap, then move on once it works.
HubSpot suggests early signals within 4 to 6 weeks and clearer results after 2
or 3 full cycles (roughly 3 to 6 months); treat those as vendor guidance, not
benchmarks.

| Evidence in the files | Stuck in | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| No profile, or profile `draft` | Express | Finish the profile |
| Brief without a concept test | Express (campaign) | Run the concept test |
| Concept test done, no formats or hooks | Tailor | Pick formats and 2 or 3 hooks |
| Plan done, status still `planning` after the start date | Amplify | Publish the first piece |
| Live, no check-in for two cycles | Evolve | Run a check-in |
| Campaign done, no lessons | Evolve | Write lessons, update the profile |
