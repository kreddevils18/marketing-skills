# Interview guide

Ask one question per message. Offer 2 to 4 options drawn from what you scouted,
recommended option first, and accept free text. Multi-select is fine for lists
(pains, outcomes, channels). Stop asking once a section has what it needs.

## Order

Order matters only where a later answer depends on an earlier one: brand and
goal first, because audience, voice and beliefs are judged against them.

1. **Brand and type.** Which brand is this, personal or product? What code goes
   into campaign names?
2. **Business goal.** What should content achieve in the next quarter:
   authority, audience growth, signups, sales? Is there a paid offer? Which
   products or results can serve as proof?
3. **Audience.** Who exactly, and where do they hang out? Offer candidate pains
   and outcomes as a multi-select, then ask for the words they use.
4. **Role and voice.** How should the brand come across: practitioner sharing
   numbers, expert teaching, product speaking? Person (I, we, product name),
   publishing language, register.
5. **Guidelines.** What must it never do? Offer common bans (hype, guru tone,
   emoji stacks, clickbait threads) as a multi-select, then ask for private
   topics that must never appear.
6. **Beliefs.** Propose 3 to 5 contrarian beliefs that follow from the goal and
   audience. For each one the user keeps, ask for the proof.
7. **Channel formats.** Ask whether they want formats from top performers on
   their main channel; see `channel-formats.md`.
8. **Voice bank.** Draft 8 to 10 questions the audience asks, with answers built
   on the beliefs. Present them as drafts for the user to rewrite.

## Turning vague answers into usable ones

| Vague answer | Follow-up |
|---|---|
| "Founders" | Which founders, at what stage, building what, on which platform? |
| "Be authentic" | Show me a sentence you would post and one you never would. |
| "We help people grow" | Grow what, measured how, and what result can you show? |
| A belief with no proof | What have you built or measured that shows it? If nothing yet, keep it as a hypothesis. |
| "Professional but friendly" | Pick two sample lines; which sounds like you? |

## When the user does not know

Offer your best inference from the scouting as the recommended option and let
them correct it. Do not block the profile on one answer: leave `[ ]`, keep
`status: draft`, and list the open items at the end.
